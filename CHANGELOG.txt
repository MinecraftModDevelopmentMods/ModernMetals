2.5.1.110021 for 1.10.2

This brings the 1.10.2 branch up to date. Use Java 8, Forge 12.18.3.2511 or later for 1.10.2, Base Metals 2.5.1.110021 and MMD OreSpawn 4.1.2.110021.

- Restored boron, beryllium and thorium, including their ores, molten forms and recipes.
- Kept titanium's rutile and magnesium recipe. Titanium ore generation is optional and starts off.
- Moved ore generation to OreSpawn 4. Existing OreSpawn settings can carry forward and retrogen stays off.
- Used KiriCattus's ore overlays so the stone in your resource pack shows through. Thorium keeps its original texture.
- Put Modern Metals settings in both the Forge Mods menu and OreSpawn's Mods list. The screens have German, Simplified Chinese and Russian translations, with regional English files also included.
- Added High and Low Fantasy and a switch for new villager trades. Restart after changing them. Clients need the same Fantasy Level as their server.
- Fixed IC2 and Mekanism processing and a Tinkers casting issue when the aluminum fluid was already registered.
- Checked all 20 ores with the Base Metals crusher registry and the baseSteam and baseElectric crusher lookups.
- Restored old door, fishing rod, horse armor, alloy ore and molten aluminum brass IDs for 0.11.0 worlds. Back up an older world before opening it with the new jars.
- Updated the build and Eclipse setup to ForgeGradle 7. The game still runs on Java 8.
