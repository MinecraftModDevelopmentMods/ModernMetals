4.0.0.121012 for 1.21.1

Ported to Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge 21.1, built on MMDLib 2.0.2 and OreSpawn 4.
Materials are MMDLib definitions in mmdlib/material/; MMDLib registers their items, blocks, arrows, armor and molten fluids.
Recipes use common (c:) tags instead of the ore dictionary. Low Fantasy now removes restricted recipes when a world loads.
Fantasy Level, villager trades and molten metal burning are set in config/modernmetals-common.toml or the in-game settings screen.
Ores generate between Y -64 and their 1.12 maximum height. Titanium ore stays optional in OreSpawn.
Integrations with mods that have no 1.21.1 release (Tinkers' Construct, Construct's Armory, IC2, Thermal Expansion, Ender IO, Thaumcraft, Dense Ores and Vein Miner) were removed. Mekanism processing forms appear when Mekanism is installed.
1.12.2 worlds cannot be upgraded to this version.

2.5.2.112021 for 1.12.2

Use the published MMDLib 1.0.0-rc3 and MMD OreSpawn 4.1.6.112021.
Checked alongside Base Metals 2.6.2.112021.
Molten metal now burns on contact with the liquid surface.
Restored missing German, Russian and Simplified Chinese translations.
Fixed black backgrounds on ore overlays so the stone texture shows through.
Old 1.12.2 worlds can load past obsolete crusher recipes with empty outputs.

2.5.1.112021 for 1.12.2

- Updated the build to ForgeGradle 7 and moved Modern Metals to the ModDev Zone namespace.
- Switched ore generation to OreSpawn 4 and added ore overlays that use the active stone texture.
- Kept titanium ore optional. Rutile and magnesium still make titanium.
- Added a settings screen in Forge and OreSpawn. Modern Metals follows Base Metals' Fantasy Level when installed with a compatible version.
- Added damage from contact with molten metal and kept ore processing in MMDLib's shared crusher registry.
- Fixed stainless steel and nichrome blend recipes while keeping the Chrome ore-dictionary alias.
- Fixed plate and door models when Base Metals isn't installed.

*Metals family wide cleanups and bugfixes
