3.0.0.113021 for 1.13.2

- Use Base Metals 3.0.3 or later when installed together, with its shared processing and mining fixes.
- Review mining levels for every metal. Galvanized steel now matches steel and iron.
- Give tools, solid metals and rock-hosted ores separate mining levels; document the material assumptions.
- Use the normal hammer mining speed for ores and storage blocks from other mods.
- Use released OreSpawn 4.1.7 in normal development and game launches.
- Fix a startup hang with other fluid-adding mods, without disabling parallel loading.
- Support a checked local OreSpawn fix in Eclipse and Gradle development launches.
- Allow steel blend directly in galvanized steel and stainless steel recipes, keeping the same yields.
- Added crafting and furnace recipe discovery, including powders already held in a saved inventory.
- Restored small-powder and small-blend smelting, storage-block crushing, recycling and plate repairs.
- Added the standalone coal-dust block cycle. Base Metals supplies the shared coal recipes when installed.
- Kept the five alloy formulas and restored material aliases, including Chrome ingredients in mixed-mod blends.
- Kept earned recipe progress through resource reloads and Fantasy Level changes.
- Fixed a startup race that could crash while reading or saving worlds with Base Metals installed.
- Fixed crackhammer mining so coal ore drops coal dust and other ores use their crushing recipes.
- Ported Modern Metals to Forge 25 and the native OreSpawn 4 provider.
- Converted old block metadata to named states, including both halves of doors and double slabs.
- Kept Modern Metals IDs and converted old filled buckets and worn equipment when loading legacy worlds.
- Converted recipes, translations and ore models to the Minecraft 1.13.2 formats.
- Kept the Forge and OreSpawn settings screens and Base Metals Fantasy Level inheritance.
